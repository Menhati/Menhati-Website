The UK government's flagship scholarship for future leaders and influencers. It funds one full
academic year of any master's programme at a UK university, in any subject — it is not limited to
particular fields.

**Deadline: 6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC.**

## Eligibility
- Citizen of a Chevening-eligible country
- At least two years of work experience (2,800 hours)
- A bachelor's degree that qualifies you for entry to a UK master's programme
- A commitment to return home for at least two years after the scholarship ends
- Apply to three different eligible UK courses and hold an unconditional offer from one of them by
  Chevening's stated deadline

## How applicants are assessed
Leadership and influence, networking ability, a clear career plan showing how studying in the UK
will let you make a positive impact, and the quality of your proposed study plan.

## Documents required
- Valid passport
- Transcripts and degree certificate
- Proof of work experience
- An unconditional offer letter from one of your three chosen universities
